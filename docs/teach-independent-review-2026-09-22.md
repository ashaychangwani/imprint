## The systemic problem and the smallest response

The existing stages repeatedly move between a narrow successful request, a public parameter contract, a dependency contract and a generated parser. Those are different claims. Research is parser-free; its runtime proof binding establishes the tested candidate and successful result, not the completeness or meaning of a reusable producer value. The final master rejection is therefore evidence that a necessary check worked, not a reason to weaken it. See [research proof binding](/Users/ashaychangwani/.codex/worktrees/imprint-master-v066-validation/src/imprint/master-teach-agents.ts:530).

Two architectural choices make this loop unnecessarily expensive:

- The initial master chooses boundaries/contracts with summaries and evidence counts. [Discovery construction](/Users/ashaychangwani/.codex/worktrees/imprint-master-v066-validation/src/imprint/replay-evidence.ts:697) strips request bodies, headers and response previews; [retained master projection](/Users/ashaychangwani/.codex/worktrees/imprint-master-v066-validation/src/imprint/master-teach-agents.ts:1271) supplies `evidenceCoverage(..., [])`, containing no cited entries. Flights 12 paired unlike recorded operation variants and repaired the boundary around minute 40.38, then the continuation representation around minute 67.43. This supports earlier evidence delivery, but does not prove that seeing a body would have prevented either mistake.
- Research, planning and compilation rewrite overlapping artifacts instead of consistently referring to an exact retained candidate and its observations. Large source outputs and fragile handoffs increase opportunities for drift. The host already stores candidate hashes and evidence; use those existing concepts rather than adding another planning layer or semantic classifier.

The coherent correction is to keep exact artifacts and observations stable, let agents inspect the few facts needed at each decision, and make malformed reports repairable without losing work. Do not replace this with a broad agent-framework rewrite, more agents, a universal state taxonomy, or another long prompt rulebook.
