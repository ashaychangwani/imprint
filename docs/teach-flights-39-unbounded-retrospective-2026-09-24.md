Flights39 started fresh on `aa2f8e4` with the combined September 4 Google Flights recording and the four requested operation groups. It ran for 120.07 minutes and exited with three ready tools (`search_flight_locations`, `get_flight_location_details`, and `search_one_way_flights`) and two not ready (`get_one_way_booking_options` and `get_date_grid`). No independent audit ran. Its isolated home and complete failed evidence are at `~/.imprint/experiments/reteach-systemic-2026-09-21/flights-39-unbounded/`.

The current runtime fixes worked: two completed host passes lacking a native acknowledgment were released after the bounded cleanup instead of stalling. The master chose distinct tool purposes and accepted a five-tool MVP plan. One-way search and location lookup published after live checks. The booking check used a fresh one-way search itinerary and its `booking_context`; both requests completed and the chain receipt passed. The independent reviewer established comparable recorded/live booking responses and found the same selected itinerary and four fare products, with a permitted live price change. Its evaluation did not finish before the recording-review deadline, so booking was not published.

The date-grid check also executed and returned a full 7×7 grid. The first semantic review found a real parser omission: it dropped the nonempty opaque token from each cell. The master recalled only that artifact. Its retained compiler repaired the parser, and the next live output visibly included `opaque_cell_data` in populated cells. That second semantic evaluation also hit the review deadline before a final verdict. The master correctly preserved its accepted plan and classified both outstanding notices as timeouts rather than new artifact defects. The coordinator then stopped because the same unresolved proof facts recurred after master review.

The exact common failure was a **single five-minute budget shared by the independent review's raw-source expectations and parser-output evaluation**. Complex recorded/live frames required several bounded inspections; by the time evaluation started, little time remained. The error text said the provider call exceeded its deadline, but the deadline was imposed by Imprint's review phase. There were no native family errors, missing response bodies, or disk failures in this run. The 90-minute whole-run limit was disabled as requested.

The general correction gives expectations and evaluation separate five-minute budgets. If either phase reaches its budget, it retries the same pending turn with retained agent conversation and frozen source expectations, backs off, and records each retry in `.recording-verification/*.retries.jsonl`. A hard whole-run deadline or cancellation still stops the review. The correction does not accept invalid proof or rerun live requests merely to retry the reviewer. Focused regression, the full 2,066-test suite, lint, type checking, and diff checks passed. The next validation must be a fresh teach on this code, not a resumed Flights39 run.
